Questions

Before you start

What is a DIY endless tow?

An endless rope loop strung between two low A-frames on the shore. One motor turns a capstan, the loop moves, and a carrier on the loop pulls the rider. Reverse the motor and the rider comes back the other way.

Is it the same as a cable park or a winch?

No. It's a small shuttle for one rider at a time, meant for foil and skim at a tow height of about 0.8 to 1.5 m over roughly 80 to 150 m. It isn't a cable park, a one-way winch or a wake tower.

What does a build cost?

The parts budget for the prototype is about $1,750 to $5,000. The parts list shows what's on the bench, and community builds show what other builders used and paid.

Can I buy one?

No. System 6.9 is a community build, not a product. The guide, the build gallery and the software are free.

Is it safe?

It's a build journal, not a certified tow system, so build and ride at your own risk. The guide starts with the safety rules: an E-stop on every session, a soft-start motor controller, guards on every nip point and a spotter for every ride.
